Toylight

Role: Solo Developer | Engine: Unity6 | Year: 2025 | Project Status: Finished

Itch page: https://pixeldemoncheems.itch.io/toylight

About the Game

Toylight is an autobattler / base defense Christmas-themed game in which you defend your Toy Factory from spooky creatures trying to ruin Christmas. You make Toys and shine them with your magical life-giving light so they can defend your Toy Factory.

Toylight is a game that I made for the Jame Gam Christmas Edition 2025, in which I won 1st Place!
The theme for this jam was "Light" and the special object is a "Bell".
